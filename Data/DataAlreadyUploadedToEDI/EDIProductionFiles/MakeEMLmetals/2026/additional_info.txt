Contributions to the 2025 revision:
MES oversaw the metals sampling program at Falling Creek Reservoir and Beaverdam Reservoir from 2014 - present, and at Carvins Cove Reservoir in 2022 and 2025. DAK and CEW served as the point people for data cleaning, analysis, and QAQC in 2025. ABP led field crew, contributed significantly to sampling, and assisted substantially with QAQC workflows. AEB, CEB, and TW contributed significantly to sampling, data digitization, and sample organization. QC, SEK, and EW contributed to fieldwork and field monitoring preparations in 2025.
